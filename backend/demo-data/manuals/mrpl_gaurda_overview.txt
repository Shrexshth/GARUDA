MRPL Overview
Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL) is an Indian Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) refinery located in Mangaluru, Karnataka. It is a Schedule ‘A’ Miniratna company and operates as a subsidiary of the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC), which is under the ownership of the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Government of India. The refinery complex operates state-of-the-art facilities processing a wide variety of crudes to produce petroleum products.

GAURDA System Overview
GAURDA (Industrial AI Workbench) is the on-premise, air-gapped AI system deployed at MRPL. It is designed to provide sovereign artificial intelligence capabilities strictly within the refinery's secure network. GAURDA assists engineering and operational staff with technical document synthesis, drafting formal Notes for Approval (NFA) in compliance with MRPL Delegation of Powers, parsing complex equipment schedules (like pump data sheets and P&IDs) through vision models, and answering operational queries using indexed standards such as OISD (Oil Industry Safety Directorate) manuals. The system utilizes local language models (like Qwen) and local Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) powered by ChromaDB to ensure no sensitive engineering data leaves the premises.
