MRPL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — SOP/MECH/P-102
Equipment: Centrifugal Pump P-102A/B (CDU Feed Pump)
Revision: 03 | Date: 2024-08-15

1. PURPOSE
To establish safe and standardized operating procedures for starting, monitoring, and shutting down centrifugal feed pump P-102A/B in the Crude Distillation Unit (CDU).

2. OPERATING PARAMETERS
| Parameter | Normal Range | Alarm Low | Alarm High | Trip |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|------------|------|
| Suction Pressure | 2.5–3.5 kg/cm² | 2.0 | 4.0 | 1.5 |
| Discharge Pressure | 18–22 kg/cm² | 16.0 | 24.0 | 14.0 |
| Flow Rate | 350–450 m³/hr | 300 | 500 | 250 |
| Bearing Temp (DE) | 55–70 °C | — | 85 | 95 |
| Bearing Temp (NDE) | 50–65 °C | — | 80 | 90 |
| Vibration (DE) | < 4.5 mm/s | — | 7.1 | 11.2 |
| Vibration (NDE) | < 4.0 mm/s | — | 6.5 | 10.0 |
| Motor Current | 180–220 A | 150 | 250 | 280 |

3. STARTUP PROCEDURE
3.1 Confirm suction valve is fully open and discharge valve is throttled to 15%.
3.2 Verify lube oil level in bearing housing is at mid-sight-glass.
3.3 Check that mechanical seal flush system is operational (pot pressure > 1.0 kg/cm² above suction).
3.4 Start the pump motor from the local control panel or DCS.
3.5 Slowly open the discharge valve to achieve rated flow within 3 minutes.
3.6 Monitor vibration, bearing temperature, and motor current for 15 minutes after start.

4. NORMAL SHUTDOWN PROCEDURE
4.1 Gradually close the discharge valve over 2 minutes.
4.2 Stop the motor after flow drops below 50 m³/hr.
4.3 Close the suction valve only if the pump is being isolated for maintenance.
4.4 Continue lube oil circulation for 15 minutes after shutdown.

5. EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN TRIGGERS
- Bearing temperature > 95°C on either end
- Vibration > 11.2 mm/s on Drive End
- Motor current > 280 A sustained for > 10 seconds
- Loss of suction pressure below 1.5 kg/cm²
- Mechanical seal failure (visible fluid leak > 60 drops/min)

6. MAINTENANCE NOTES
- Impeller clearance to be checked every 8000 running hours per API 610 Section 8.3.
- Mechanical seal replacement interval: 12,000 hours or on failure.
- Coupling alignment to be verified after any maintenance involving bearing removal.
