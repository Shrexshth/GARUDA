OISD STANDARD 116 — FIRE PROTECTION FACILITIES FOR PETROLEUM REFINERIES

1. SCOPE
This standard covers fire protection facilities in petroleum refineries and gas processing plants operated by the oil industry. It applies to new installations and major modifications to existing facilities.

2. FIRE WATER SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
2.1 The fire water network shall be designed to maintain a minimum residual pressure of 7.0 kg/cm² at the hydraulically most remote hydrant, with all simultaneously required water demands flowing.
2.2 Fire water pump capacity shall be at least 3600 m³/hr for a refinery with a crude throughput exceeding 6 MMTPA.
2.3 Fire water storage shall be sufficient for a minimum of 4 hours of continuous operation.

3. FOAM SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS
3.1 All floating roof tanks storing flammable liquids with a flash point below 65°C shall be provided with fixed foam systems.
3.2 Foam concentrate storage capacity shall be adequate for a minimum of 55 minutes of continuous operation.
3.3 The foam application rate shall not be less than 12.2 liters per minute per square meter of tank area.

5. INSPECTION AND TESTING
5.1 Fire water pumps shall be test-run weekly for a minimum of 30 minutes each.
5.2 Fire hydrants shall be inspected quarterly and tested annually.
5.3 All fire detection and alarm systems shall be tested semi-annually.
5.4 Foam concentrate quality shall be tested annually by an approved laboratory.
